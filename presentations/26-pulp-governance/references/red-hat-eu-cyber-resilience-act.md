# Red Hat and the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)

- **Publisher:** Red Hat
- **Updated:** March 4, 2026
- **Source:** <https://www.redhat.com/en/red-hat-eu-cyber-resilience-act>
- **Accessed:** November 6, 2026

## Summary

Red Hat describes its approach to preparing products and customers for the EU
Cyber Resilience Act (CRA). The page highlights product lifecycle transparency,
manufacturer accountability, vulnerability reduction, secure-by-design
practices, vulnerability reporting, and software supply chain information such
as software bills of materials. It also discusses Red Hat's participation in
standards work and its role supporting upstream open-source projects.

## Use in the draft

Use this page as Red Hat's public industry perspective on CRA readiness and
open-source stewardship. It is not a substitute for the CRA's legal text or
project-specific governance policies.
