# EU Cyber Resilience Act Stewardship Guidelines for Red Hat Supported Open Source Projects

- **Publisher:** Red Hat Customer Portal
- **Last updated:** June 12, 2026
- **Source:** <https://access.redhat.com/security/eu-cyber-resilience-act-stewardship-guidelines>
- **Accessed:** November 6, 2026

## Summary

Red Hat's guidance describes its approach to supporting selected upstream open
source projects under the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA). It identifies Pulp as
one of fifteen projects for which Red Hat considers itself a steward, in the
Light Stewardship category. The page outlines proposed stewardship principles,
security and vulnerability handling practices, reporting coordination, and
implementation guidance for project maintainers.

## Use in the draft

Use this as Red Hat's published stewardship guidance and stated position about
Pulp. Pair it with the CRA itself and Pulp's own policies when evaluating legal
responsibilities or project governance decisions.
